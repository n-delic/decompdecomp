fix(rom-join): skip the mirrored fold when a match lands mid-sync

apply_remote_layout snapshots the guest's functions, then joins an
over-split head and its tails back inside a later transaction. A match
could land on the head between the snapshot and that write (e.g. the
head matching at its short extent while join_saved_functions reads the
ROM), and the fold went ahead on the stale snapshot -- leaving the head
status='matched' at the joined size but with matched_source for the
short piece, and the tail deleted.

Re-read the head and tails inside the transaction and skip the whole
fold -- the write, the tail deletes and the symbol resize -- when any
piece is now matched or gone. The new test lands the match during the
join and fails without the guard.

Also correct the join gate's wording in the workflow guide and the
join_functions tool help: a join lands once three agents of two models
agree (not two), matching agents/api.py.
